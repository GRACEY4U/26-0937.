✨ Detailed Descriptions

Soft Glam: A blend of neutral mattes with a pop of shimmer on the lids. It’s polished but not overdone—perfect for office or brunch.

Bold Lip: Let your lips do the talking! Pair a classic red or berry stain with groomed brows and a touch of mascara for instant glamour.

Cut Crease: This dramatic eye uses concealer to carve out the crease, creating depth. Ideal for photoshoots or nights out.

No-Makeup Look: All about skin-like finishes. Use a dewy foundation, cream blush, and curled lashes for a fresh-faced glow.

Graphic Liner: Break the rules with geometric shapes and neon liners. It’s an artistic expression that turns eyes into art.
