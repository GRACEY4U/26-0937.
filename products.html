🔍 How to Choose

Dry Skin: Look for hydrating ingredients like hyaluronic acid. Dewey finishes are your friend. Avoid heavy powders.

Oily Skin: Matte, oil-free formulas with a velvet finish will keep shine at bay. Don't skip primer!

Sensitive Skin: Mineral-based, fragrance-free products reduce irritation. Always patch test.

Budget Tips: Drugstore brands now rival luxury. All prices are in Kenyan Shillings (KES) with thousands separated by commas.

Sustainability: Refillable compacts and clean beauty are on the rise. Support brands with eco-initiatives.
