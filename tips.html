🌟 Insider Pro Tips

💧 Tip 1: Skin Prep is Everything

Never apply makeup to dry skin. Exfoliate weekly and always use a moisturizer suited to your skin type. Hydrated skin = glowing makeup that lasts.

🖌️ Tip 2: Less is More with Foundation

Start with a pea-sized amount and build coverage only where needed. The center of the face often needs more, while the perimeter needs blending.

👁️ Tip 3: The Eyebrow Rule

Your brows should be sisters, not twins. Fill in sparse areas with hairlike strokes, and set with a clear gel for a polished look.

✨ Tip 4: Highlighter Placement

Apply highlighter on the high points of your face: cheekbones, brow bone, cupid's bow, and the inner corner of eyes. Avoid textured areas.

🌙 Tip 5: Nighttime Removal

Never sleep in your makeup! Use a double-cleanse method: oil-based cleanser first, then water-based. Your skin will thank you.

✅ Brush Cleaning Checklist (Unordered List)

  • Deep clean brushes every 7-10 days
  • Use lukewarm water and gentle soap
  • Reshape bristles and dry flat
  • Sanitize powders and palettes weekly
  • Replace sponges every 3 months

⏳ Perfect Base Routine (Ordered List)

  1. Primer (let it sit for 2 minutes)
  2. Color corrector if needed
  3. Foundation (stippling motion)
  4. Concealer in a triangle shape
  5. Set with powder only in T-zone
